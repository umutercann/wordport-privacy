Türkçe

Wordport, havalimanının bagaj bandındaki valizleri genel kültür sorularıyla açtığın bir oyun. Harf tekerlekli kilide cevabı yazarsın, açılan valizden çıkan eşya şehrin albümüne girer.

English

Wordport is a game about opening the suitcases left on an airport baggage belt by answering general knowledge questions. You spell the answer on a letter-wheel lock, and what comes out goes into the city's album. The game is in Turkish for now.

Contact

For questions, bug reports or ideas: umutercan550@gmail.com. When reporting a bug, please include your device, its OS version and the version number shown on the game's Settings screen.

Common questions

I bought Ad-free but it is not there. Go to Settings › "Satın alımları geri yükle" (Restore purchases), signed in with the same App Store or Google Play account.

A question has a wrong answer. Send us the question and the answer; we will check the source and fix it.

Where is my save? Only on your device; there is no account. Uninstalling the app deletes it.
